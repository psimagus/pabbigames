Reversi (Othello)

Reversi is a two-player strategy game played with black and white discs. This uses the familiar standard 8×8 form, also widely known as Othello. Black moves first.

Goal

Finish with more discs of your colour than your opponent. The game ends when the board is full or neither player has a legal move.

Placing and turning discs

Place a disc on an empty square so that one or more straight lines of opposing discs are trapped between the new disc and another disc of your colour. Lines may run horizontally, vertically or diagonally. Every trapped disc turns to your colour.

A move must turn at least one opposing disc. Green dots mark your legal squares. The last disc placed has a bright yellow outline; all discs it turned retain a softer yellow glow until the next move.

Passing

If you have no legal move, your turn passes automatically. If your opponent can move, they play again. If neither side can move, the game ends and the discs are counted.

Tips

Corners can never be turned and are extremely valuable. Avoid surrendering the squares immediately beside an empty corner. Taking many discs early is not necessarily good: restricting your opponent's legal moves is often more important.
