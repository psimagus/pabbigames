Hnefatafl - The Viking Game

1000 years old. Vikings played this before chess existed. It is an unequal battle: the King and his 12 White defenders start in the centre, surrounded by 24 Brown attackers.

Goal

White wins if the King (with crown) reaches any corner square. Brown wins if they capture the King.

How pieces move

All pieces move like a rook in chess: any number of squares horizontally or vertically, no jumping over pieces. Only the King may land on the throne (centre) or corners. Brown may not land on the throne.

Captures

Capture by sandwiching: move so an enemy piece is trapped between your piece and another of your pieces, or between your piece and a corner / throne. This is not like chess, you do not capture by landing on someone.

The King is captured when surrounded on all four sides by Brown. Beside the throne, the empty throne counts as the fourth hostile side.

Rules in this version

- Brown always moves first
- In a human game, the creator plays Brown and shares the four-character code; the player who joins is White
- Against Cassie, choose a difficulty and then choose whether to play White or Brown
- Free Play lets two people share this phone, or one person explore both sides; take turns moving Brown and White
- In online and Cassie games, you cannot move your opponent's pieces
- Corners are dark brown, throne is light brown

Tips

White: open routes early and contest corner blockades; a tight centre can become trapped. Brown: close escape routes and build a diagonal line of three pieces to seal a corner.
